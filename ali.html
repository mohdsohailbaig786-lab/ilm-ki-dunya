Hazrat Ali Radiyallahu Anhu: ilm, shujaat aur khidmat

27 November 2026 · 2 min padhai

Yeh Ahle Sunnat wal Jamaat ke aam taarrufi daire mein likha gaya ibtidai draft hai. Tashheer se pehle Sunni aalim ki jaanch baqi hai. Yeh fatwa ya tafseeli deeni rehnumai ka badal nahi.

Hazrat Ali Radiyallahu Anhu chauthe khalifa, Rasoolullah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke chachazad bhai aur damaad hain. Ahle Sunnat wal Jamaat Ahl-e-Bayt aur tamam Sahaba ka adab karte hain.

Makkah, Madinah aur Kufa

Hazrat Ali Radiyallahu Anhu taqreeban 600 CE mein Makkah Mukarramah mein paida hue. Bachpan hi se Rasoolullah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke qareeb rahe aur ibtidai Islam qabool karne walon mein shamil hain. Unki khilafat 656 se 661 CE tak rahi. Is ahd mein Kufa markaz bana. 661 CE mein Kufa mein shahadat hui.

Khaybar ki mustanad riwayat

Sahih al-Bukhari 3701 mein Khaybar ke mauqe par Rasoolullah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki taraf se Hazrat Ali Radiyallahu Anhu ko jhanda dene ka zikr hai. Is riwayat mein dawat aur hidayat ki ahmiyat bhi samne aati hai. Shujaat ka matlab sirf jismi taqat nahi; zimmedari ke saath haq par qayam rehna bhi hai.

Ilm ke saath adab

Hazrat Ali Radiyallahu Anhu ilm, faisla karne ki salahiyat aur shujaat ke liye yaad kiye jate hain. Unke naam se social media par har quote mustanad nahi hota. Kisi baat ko unki taraf mansoob karne se pehle asal kitaab aur hawala dekhein. Ahl-e-Bayt aur Sahaba ka zikr muhabbat aur ehtiyat se karein; tareekhi ikhtilafat ko tauheen ya firqawarana bahas ka zariya na banayein. Yeh mazmoon mushtarka aur mashhoor taarrufi maloomat tak mehdood hai.
