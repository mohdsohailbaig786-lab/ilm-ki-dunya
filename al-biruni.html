Al-Biruni: aasman se Zameen ki paimaish

27 November 2026 · 2 min padhai

Zameen ka size jaanne ke liye poori Zameen par safar karna lazmi hai? Al-Biruni ka kaam dikhata hai ke geometry aur ehtiyat se li hui measurements kitni kaam aa sakti hain.

Zamana aur jagah

Al-Biruni 973 CE mein Khwarazm ke ilaqe mein paida hue. Unka ilmi safar Ghazna aur Hindustan tak phela. Wafat ki tareekh par ikhtilaf hai; aksar 1048 diya jata hai, jabki Britannica taqreeban 1052 batata hai. Woh Muslim scholar the jinhon ne astronomy, mathematics aur geography par kaam kiya.

Pahar, ufaq aur geometry

Ek mashhoor tareeqe mein pahar ki unchai aur uski choti se nazar aane wale horizon ka neeche jhukne wala angle kaam aata hai. Spherical Earth ki geometry se radius nikala ja sakta hai. Yeh andaaza pahar ki unchai aur angle ki durusti par depend karta hai; hawa mein roshni ka murhna bhi measurement ko mutasir karta hai. Sirf aankh ka andaaza kaafi nahi.

Ilm ki lambi kadi

Unhon ne sitaron ki positions, waqt aur geographic coordinates par kaam kiya. Kitab al-Hind mein Hindustan ki zubaan, ilm aur riwayat ka mutalaa kiya. Unka kaam mathematical geography aur astronomy ki tareekh ka aham hissa bana. Unhein har modern device ka seedha inventor kehna durust nahi; unki asal misaal sawal, hisaab aur paimaish ko jorna hai.

Aaj ka sawal: Pahar ki unchai mein ghalti ho to Earth radius ke hisaab par kya asar hoga?

Apna ilm test karo

Pehle khud sochein, phir jawab khol kar jaanchein.

2. Radius ke tareeqe mein kya naapte hain?
Jawab dekhein

3. Kitab al-Hind kis mutalae se judi hai?
Jawab dekhein

Hindustan ke ilm aur muashray ke mutalae se.
