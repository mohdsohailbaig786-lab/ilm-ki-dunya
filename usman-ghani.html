Hazrat Usman Ghani Radiyallahu Anhu: Quran ki khidmat

27 November 2026 · 2 min padhai

Yeh Ahle Sunnat wal Jamaat ke aam taarrufi daire mein likha gaya ibtidai draft hai. Tashheer se pehle Sunni aalim ki jaanch baqi hai. Yeh fatwa ya tafseeli deeni rehnumai ka badal nahi.

Hazrat Usman Ghani Radiyallahu Anhu teesre khalifa aur Rasoolullah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke mohtaram sahabi hain. Unki khidmat mein Quran ke mustanad masahif ki naql aur unhein mukhtalif ilaqon tak pahunchana bohot aham hai.

Mukhtasar taarruf

Hazrat Usman Ghani Radiyallahu Anhu Quraysh se the aur ibtidai musalmanon mein shamil hue. Makkah Mukarramah aur Madinah Munawwarah unki zindagi ke aham markaz hain. Unki khilafat 644 se 656 CE tak rahi. 656 CE mein Madinah Munawwarah mein shahadat hui. Is taarrufi draft mein fitnon ki tafseel ya ilzamat ki bahas shamil nahi.

Masahif ki naql kyun hui?

Sahih al-Bukhari 4987 mein qiraat ke ikhtilaf ka andesha samne aane par mustanad suhuf se masahif tayyar karwane ka zikr hai. Hazrat Usman Ghani Radiyallahu Anhu ne committee ko zimmedari di. Yeh pehle se mehfooz Quran ki naql aur ummat ko mustanad matn par jama rakhne ki khidmat thi; nayi wahi ya naya matn banana nahi tha.

Ilm ki hifazat ki seekh

Quran ki khidmat ka zikr karte waqt asal riwayat aur durust alfaaz aham hain. Naql, jama aur wahi ke darmiyan farq samjhein. Aam kitaab ki proofreading ki misaal sirf ehtiyat samajhne ke liye ho sakti hai; Quran Allah Taala ka kalam hai aur iska maqam aam kitaab jaisa nahi. Hazrat Usman Ghani Radiyallahu Anhu ka zikr poore adab ke saath karein aur tafseeli sawal Sunni aalim se poochhein.

Aaj ka sawal: Mustanad matn ki naql mein apni taraf se lafz badalna kyun ghalat hoga?

Apna ilm test karo

Pehle khud sochein, phir jawab khol kar jaanchein.

1. Teesre khalifa kaun hain?
Jawab dekhein

Hazrat Usman Ghani Radiyallahu Anhu.

2. Masahif ki naql ka hawala?
Jawab dekhein

Sahih al-Bukhari 4987.

3. Kya yeh nayi wahi thi?
Jawab dekhein

Nahin; pehle se mehfooz Quran ke masahif ki naql thi.
