Ala Hazrat (RA) ki Kitabein Online: Free Kutub Khana

27 November 2026 · 5 min padhai

Ilm ki duniya mein achhi kitab tak pahunchna ek khoobsurat shuruat hai. Imam Ahmed Raza Khan (RA), jinhein adab se Ala Hazrat kaha jata hai, Bareilly se talluq rakhne wale Ahle Sunnat wal Jamaat ke bade aalim the. Unki tehreeron mein fiqh, aqeedah, naatiya shairi aur doosre ilmi mauzuaat milte hain. Unka likha hua sarmaya bahut wasee hai: sawanih nigar aur bibliographies mukhtalif tareeqon se taqreeban 350 se 1000+ tak tasaneef ginte hain. Risalon, majmuon aur editions ki alag ginti ki wajah se ek hi number ko aakhri faisla na samjhein.

Yeh Ala Hazrat Kutub Khana ek aasaan raasta-numa hai, naya digital edition nahin. Neeche kitabon, risalon, tarjumo aur mauzuaati index ke links ek jagah rakhe gaye hain. Maqsad yeh hai ke naya padhne wala bhi samajh sake ke kis zaroorat ke liye kahan jana behtar rahega. Har link ke saath chhoti si pehchan hai; download ya reading ki sahulat asal website par milegi.

Padhai kahan se shuru karein?

Agar pehli baar dekh rahe hain to broad starting point par jayein: wahan 206 risalat, Fatawa editions, Kanzul Iman aur doosri kitabon ki raah milti hai. Kisi khaas unwan ki talash ho to bibliography madadgar hai. Fatawa-e-Razvia ke liye 30 jild wala majmua diya gaya hai. Kisi masle ka lafz mil jana poori ibarat samajh lene ke barabar nahin; jild, safha aur sawal ka siyaq bhi dekhein.

Rozmarra deeni masail mein apni surat-e-haal ka hukm khud lagane ke bajaye mustanad Sunni aalim se rehnumai lein. Is safhe ka kaam kitabon tak pahunch dena hai, fard ki zaroorat ke mutabiq fatwa dena nahin. Padhte waqt kitab ka naam, musannif, nashir aur edition note karna ek achhi aadat hai. Is se baad mein hawala dhoondhna aur kisi ustad se baat karna aasaan hota hai.

Adabi zauq ke liye Hadaiq-e-Bakhshish naatiya kalam ka majmua hai, jismein Nabi-e-Karim ﷺ ki shaan mein aqeedat ka izhar milta hai. English padhne walon ke liye translations ka alag raasta hai. Hisaab aur falakiyat mein dilchaspi ho to manuscripts dekhein; purane khat aur istilahon ko samajhne ke liye sabr aur kisi maahir ki madad darkar ho sakti hai.

Dhiyan rakhein

Kanzul Iman ke saath milne wale tafseer notes, jaise Khazain-ul-Irfan aur Noor-ul-Irfan, doosre ulama ke hain; unhein Ala Hazrat (RA) ki apni tafseer na kahein. Tarjuma, tafseer aur editor ke notes ko alag pehchanna durust nisbat aur ilmi adab ka hissa hai.

Free download padhne ki sahulat hai; is se modern editions ka copyright khatam nahin hota. Dobara chhapne, bechne ya online upload karne se pehle mutalliq edition ki ijazat aur sharaait dekhein. Files aur apps ki dastiyabi waqt ke saath badal sakti hai; app install karne se pehle uski permissions bhi parh lein.
