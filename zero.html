Zero ka ijaad: duniya ko sab se bara tohfa

24 November 2026

Khali tokri mein kitne aam hain? Jawab zero hai. Bachcha bhi keh dega. Lekin “kuch nahi” ko ek nishaan dena, use digits ke darmiyan jagah dena aur phir uske saath hisaab ke qawaid banana insaani soch ka bahut bada safar tha. Zero ki history ek genius ke ek din ke idea ki seedhi kahani nahi. University of St Andrews ke MacTutor archive se maloom hota hai ke placeholder aur number ke taur par zero alag marahil mein samne aaya. Hindustan ka khas tohfa decimal place-value system ke saath zero ko ek mazboot mathematical kirdar dena tha.

1. Khali jagah ko likhna kyun zaroori hua?

25 aur 205 ko dekhein. Dono mein 2 aur 5 hain, magar beech ka zero poori quantity badal deta hai. Decimal place-value notation mein har jagah pichhli jagah se das guna value rakhti hai: ones, tens, hundreds aur aage. 205 ka matlab do hundreds, koi tens nahi aur paanch ones. Zero batata hai ke beech ki place maujood hai magar usmein ginti ki koi unit nahi. Agar yeh jagah saaf na ho to reader ka andaza hi hisaab badal sakta hai.

Ek dukandaar ke register mein 2,005 rupaye aur 205 rupaye ka farq bahut practical hai. Symbol apne aap mein kuch nahi dikhata, phir bhi galat jagah lagne se amount das guna ya sau guna ho sakta hai. Isi mein place value ki taqat hai: thode digits se bahut bade numbers likhe ja sakte hain. Har naye number ke liye naya naam ya nishaan invent karne ki zaroorat nahi. Addition aur multiplication ke school wale column methods isi position ki regularity se aasaan hote hain.

MacTutor ke mutabiq Babylonians ka sexagesimal, ya base-60, place-value system bhi is mushkil se guzra. Pehle khali jagah par bharosa karna padta tha; baad mein placeholder signs aaye. Magar placeholder hona aur zero ko ek independent number samajhna ek jaisa nahi. Maya civilisation mein bhi zero ka alag, independent istemal milta hai. Isliye imaandaar history yeh nahi kehti ke duniya mein kisi ne khali position ka idea Hindustan se pehle kabhi socha hi nahi tha.

2. Hindustan mein number banne ka safar

Indian numerals ki shapes aur notation waqt ke saath badli. Aaj ka chhota gol 0 aur phone par likhe modern digits purane zamane se bilkul isi font mein nahi chale aa rahe. MacTutor Brahmi numerals aur baad ki developments ko ek lambi evolution ke taur par samjhata hai. Yeh distinction important hai: numeral ka shape, place-value system aur zero ke arithmetic rules teen related magar alag cheezein hain. Kisi ek inscription mein circle mil jana apne aap mein poore modern mathematics ke ek hi waqt mein tayyar ho jane ka saboot nahi.

Is history ka aham naam Brahmagupta hai. Unhone 628 CE mein Brahmasphutasiddhanta likha. MacTutor ke biographical account ke mutabiq yeh 25 chapters par mushtamil kaam tha aur Bhillamala, aaj ke Bhinmal se associated tha. Brahmagupta ne zero aur negative numbers ke saath calculation ke qawaid par wazeh discussion ki. Ek number ko khud se subtract karne par zero milna, zero add karne se number ka wahi rehna aur kisi number ko zero se multiply karne par zero aana jaise rules ne “khali sign” ko mathematical system mein jagah di.

Magar purane scholar ki tareef ka matlab uske har rule ko modern standard se durust kehna nahi. Brahmagupta ki division by zero ki treatment mein masail the. MacTutor batata hai ke unhone zero divided by zero ko zero kaha, jo aaj ke ordinary arithmetic mein durust nahi mana jata. History ki khoobsurti yahi hai: bade ideas bhi sawalon, adhoori samajh aur baad ki durusti ke zariye mazboot hote hain. Hum unki ahmiyat samajh sakte hain bina unhe har modern theorem ka pehle se jaanne wala banaye.

Division ko multiplication se check karein. 6 divided by 3 ka jawab 2 hai, kyunki 3 times 2 se 6 milta hai. Ab 6 divided by 0 ke liye kaunsa number chahiye jise zero se multiply karne par 6 aaye? Aisa ordinary number nahi. Aur 0 divided by 0 mein ek unique jawab nahi milta, kyunki har number ko zero se multiply karne par zero hi hota hai. Isi liye calculator ka error koi zidd nahi: sawal hi ordinary division ke defined rules mein fit nahi baithta.

3. Pathar, kitaab aur zubaan ke raaste

Gwalior ki 876 CE ki inscription Indian zero ki ek mashhoor dated misaal hai. MacTutor usmein 187 by 270 hastas ke garden aur 50 garlands ka zikr batata hai. Humein yahan modern metre mein zabardasti exact conversion nahi karna chahiye: purane length units ke context alag ho sakte hain. Is example ki asli ahmiyat yeh hai ke zero sirf abstract philosophical baat nahi raha tha; land, gifts aur real record-keeping ke numbers mein uska istemal dikh raha hai.

Isko “duniya ka sabse pehla zero, final proof” keh dena theek nahi. Manuscripts aur inscriptions ki dating, unki copying aur unmein numeral ke function par research hoti rehti hai. Bakhshali manuscript ka naam aksar headlines mein aata hai, magar manuscript ke material ki date aur likhe gaye mathematical idea ki date ko seedha ek hi baat nahi samajhna chahiye. Ek educational article ka kaam exciting headline ke saath uncertainty bhi bachana hai. Securely dated example aur earliest possible example do alag claims hain.

Indian calculation methods Islamic scholarly duniya tak pahunche. Al-Khwarizmi ke Hindu reckoning par kaam aur baad ki Arabic aur Latin transmission ne in methods ko door tak le jane mein kirdar ada kiya. Europe mein Fibonacci jaise writers ne Hindu-Arabic numerals ko samjhaya, magar adoption har jagah ek saath nahi hui. Purane counting tools, commercial habits aur notation ki aadatein badalne mein waqt lagta hai. Achha idea hona aur poori society ka use apna lena do alag historical processes hain.

4. Aaj ka zero: chhota sign, bada infrastructure

Bank balance, mobile number, science ki measurements aur computer notation mein zero har jagah nazar aata hai. Computer ka binary system 0 aur 1 use karta hai; iska matlab yeh nahi ke Brahmagupta ne modern computer invent kar diya tha. Sahi connection yeh hai ke precise symbolic number systems ne aane wali mathematics aur technology ke liye powerful bunyaad di. Har historical contribution ko uske apne context mein dekhna uski qadr kam nahi, zyada karta hai.

Zero ek reference point bhi ban sakta hai, jaise number line par positive aur negative values ke darmiyan. Lekin har jagah zero ka physical matlab “poori tarah kuch nahi” nahi hota. Misal ke taur par zero degree Celsius ka matlab heat ka absolute khatma nahi. Yeh lesson pure article ko jorta hai: symbol samajhne ke liye system samajhna padta hai. Agli baar register mein ek chhota 0 likhein to yaad rakhein ke uske peeche kai civilisations ka tajruba aur Hindustan ki bahut gehri mathematical mehnat chhupi hui hai.
