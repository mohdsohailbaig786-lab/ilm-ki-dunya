Ibn Sina: tib aur Al-Qanun fil-Tibb

27 November 2026 · 2 min padhai

Bukhar, dard aur ilaaj ko samajhne ke liye bikhri maloomat ko tarteeb dena bhi ek bada ilmi kaam hai. Ibn Sina isi kaam ke liye tareekh mein numayan hain.

Zamana aur ilaqa

Ibn Sina taqreeban 980 CE mein Bukhara ke qareeb Afshana mein paida hue aur 1037 CE mein Hamadan mein wafat pai. Yeh aaj ke Uzbekistan aur Iran ke ilaqe hain. Woh Muslim tabib aur musannif the; Latin riwayat mein Avicenna ke naam se mashhoor hue.

Al-Qanun fil-Tibb kya tha?

Al-Qanun fil-Tibb paanch kitabon par mushtamil tibbi encyclopedia tha. Is mein jism, bimariyon, dawaon aur ilaaj ki maloomat ko munazzam kiya gaya. Ibn Sina ne pehle ke Greek aur doosre tibbi ilm ko bhi jama kiya; yeh sab kuch ek shakhs ki nayi ijaad nahi tha. Bimari ki alamat ko dekhna aur mukhtalif halat ka muqabla karna is riwayat ka aham hissa tha.

Baad ki science par asar

Qanun ka Latin tarjuma Europe tak pahuncha aur sadiyon tak tibbi taleem mein padha gaya. Is ne medical knowledge ko jama karne aur padhane mein madad di. Magar tareekhi ahmiyat aur aaj ka durust ilaaj alag baatein hain: us daur ke kai nazariyat modern medicine mein qabool nahi. Aaj dawa ya tashkhees ke liye qualified doctor aur nayi medical evidence zaroori hain.

Aaj ka sawal: Medical kitaab ko munazzam chapters mein rakhna student ki kaise madad karta hai?

Apna ilm test karo

Pehle khud sochein, phir jawab khol kar jaanchein.

1. Ibn Sina kis field mein mashhoor the?
Jawab dekhein

Tib aur medicine.

2. Unki mashhoor tibbi kitaab?
Jawab dekhein

Al-Qanun fil-Tibb.

3. Kya purana nuskha aaj bina jaanch lena chahiye?
Jawab dekhein

Nahin; aaj ke ilaaj ke liye doctor aur modern evidence zaroori hain.
