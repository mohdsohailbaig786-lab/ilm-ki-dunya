QR Code kaise kaam karta hai? Chhote squares ka bada raaz

26 November 2026 · 4 min padhai

Dukaan par payment ho ya restaurant ka menu, ek chhota sa square phone ko foran nayi maloomat tak pahuncha deta hai. Lekin QR Code ke andar koi nanhi website nahi baithi hoti! Yeh data ko siyah aur safed khanon ke pattern mein likhne ka tareeqa hai. Scanner us pattern ko padh kar uska matlab nikalta hai. Aaiye is rozmarra technology ko uske banane walon, DENSO WAVE, ki explanations se samjhein.

Factory se phone tak ka safar

QR ka matlab Quick Response hai. DENSO WAVE, jo us waqt DENSO Corporation ka division tha, ne 1994 mein QR Code release kiya. Shuru mein automobile industry ne production aur shipping jaise kaamon mein ise apnaya. Yani yeh sirf mobile payments ke liye paida nahi hua tha. Japan mein QR padhne wale mobile phones ne 2002 mein aam logon tak iske istemal ko phailane mein madad ki.

Purana barcode aam taur par ek simt mein information rakhta hai. QR do dimensions, yani lambai aur chaudai, mein pattern istemal karta hai. Isko yun samjhein: ek line mein likhi baat ke muqable mein poora safha mil gaya. Yeh sirf samjhane ki misaal hai; asal capacity encoding aur design par depend karti hai.

Teen bade squares kyun?

Aam QR ke teen konon mein nazar aane wale bade squares position detection patterns hain. Yeh reader ko code ki jagah aur orientation samajhne mein madad dete hain. DENSO ki history ke mutabiq, developers ne printed material ke patterns ko khoob jaancha. Unhone black aur white widths ka 1:1:3:1:1 ratio chuna, jo doosri printing se alag pehchana ja sake. Isi tarah scanner mukhtalif angles se code ko pehchan sakta hai.

Har code mein barabar jagah nahi

DENSO ki version guide ke mutabiq, standard QR versions 1 se 40 tak hain. Version 1 mein 21 × 21 modules aur Version 40 mein 177 × 177 modules hote hain. Module ek chhota siyah ya safed khana hai. Har agle version mein har side par 4 modules badhte hain. Zyada data ke liye zyada khane chahiye ho sakte hain; har QR ki capacity ek jaisi nahi hoti.

Thoda kharab ho kar bhi kaise padhta hai?

QR mein Reed-Solomon error correction ke zariye extra data shamil kiya jata hai. Isse kuch data kharab hone par information dobara hasil ki ja sakti hai. Chaar levels L, M, Q aur H hain. DENSO ke mutabiq, aam istemal wala Level M total codewords ke taqreeban 15 percent ki restoration capability rakhta hai. Ek codeword 8 bits ka hota hai.

Iska matlab yeh nahi ke tasveer ka koi bhi 15 percent hissa mita dein aur scan lazman ho jayega. Jagah aur nuksan ki qisam aham hain. Zyada correction bhi muft nahi: uske liye jagah chahiye, isliye data aur symbol size ka tawazun rakhna padta hai. Agli baar scan karte waqt yaad rakhein: in seedhe khanon ke peeche pehchan, hisaab aur ghalti sudharne ki engineering saath kaam karti hai.

Aaj ka sawal: Agar extra correction data bhi jagah leta hai, to bahut zyada maloomat aur zyada mazbooti ek saath rakhne ke liye code ko kya badalna padega?
