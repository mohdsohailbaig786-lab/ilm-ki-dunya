Crane aur pulley: kam force se bhaari bojh

27 November 2026 · 2 min padhai

Construction site par crane ka hook bhaari load uthata hai. Pulley system force ko aasaan bana sakta hai, magar load uthane ka total kaam gaayab nahi hota.

Fixed aur movable pulley

Pulley grooved wheel hai jis par rope ya cable chalti hai. Ek fixed pulley sirf force ki direction badal sakti hai: neeche kheench kar load upar uthta hai. Ideal single fixed pulley mechanical advantage 1 deti hai. Movable pulley load ke saath chalti hai aur supporting rope segments bojh baant sakte hain.

Do rasse, aadhi force?

Ideal system mein agar do vertical rope segments load ko support kar rahe hon to tension har segment mein aadha load sambhalti hai. Mechanical advantage 2 ho sakti hai: 100 newton ke load ke liye ideal pull 50 newton. Magar load 1 metre uthane ke liye rope taqreeban 2 metre kheenchni padegi. Friction aur pulley ka apna weight asal required force badhate hain.

Crane sirf pulley nahi

Crane mein motor, winch drum, brakes, boom, counterweight aur mazboot support bhi hote hain. Boom jitna door nikle, overturning moment utna badh sakta hai; isi liye rated load chart aham hai. Sirf pulleys gin kar crane ki safe capacity tay nahi hoti. Kabhi latakte bojh ke neeche na khade hon. Lifting plan aur operation trained professionals ka kaam hai.

Aaj ka sawal: Pulley force aadhi kar de to rope ko zyada door kyun kheenchna padta hai?

Apna ilm test karo

Pehle khud sochein, phir jawab khol kar jaanchein.

1. Fixed pulley ka bunyadi faida?
Jawab dekhein

Force ki direction badalna.

2. Do supporting segments ka ideal advantage?
Jawab dekhein

2, agar arrangement aur directions munasib hon.

3. Crane ki capacity sirf pulley count se milti hai?
Jawab dekhein

Nahin; boom, radius, stability aur load chart bhi aham hain.
