Islam aur Adab

Ilm hasil karna Islam mein kitna zaroori hai

24 November 2026

Ilm sirf imtihaan ke marks, degree ya mushkil alfaaz yaad karne ka naam nahi. Islam mein ilm insaan ko apne Rab ki pehchan, ibadat ka sahi tareeqa aur logon ke huqooq samajhne mein madad deta hai. Mainstream Sunni taleem mein ilm aur amal ka gehra rishta hai: jo seekha, use niyyat, akhlaq aur zimmedari mein bhi nazar aana chahiye. SeekersGuidance ke taleemi jawabat aur adab ke mazameen is baat ko wazeh karte hain ke har shakhs ki zaroorat ka ilm aur poori community ki zaroorat ka ilm dono aham hain. Chaliye isko rozmarra zindagi ki zubaan mein samajhte hain.

1. Iqra: seekhne ki taraf pehla ishara

Quran ki pehli wahi ki ibtida “Iqra” se hui, jiska aam tarjuma “padho” ya “recite karo” kiya jata hai. Yeh Surah al-Alaq ke shuru ke paigham ka hissa hai. Wahi ke is aaghaz ka Ghaar-e-Hira se taalluq bayan kiya jata hai. Is mein ilm ko insaani ghuroor ka zariya nahi, Allah ki taraf tawajjoh ke saath jora gaya hai. Hum yahan kisi shakhs ki depiction ya tasveer ke baghair sirf taleemi paigham ki baat kar rahe hain: seekhna ek sanjeeda aur izzat wala amal hai.

Iqra ko sirf modern school ki textbook tak mehdood karna bhi adhoora hoga. Wahi ka apna deeni context hai. Saath hi is se taleem, samajh aur zubaan ki ahmiyat par sochne ka darwaza khulta hai. Jo insaan apne deen ki bunyadi baat samajhna chahta hai, use sawal karna, sunna aur qualified teachers se seekhna padta hai. Sirf family ki aadat ya internet par mil gaya ek short clip hamesha poori rehnumai nahi ban sakta.

Ek mashhoor hadith ka mafhoom hai ke ilm hasil karna har Muslim par farz hai. Yahan hum mafhoom bayan kar rahe hain, exact Arabic wording ya koi hadith number pesh nahi kar rahe. Sunni teachers isko zaroori deeni ilm ke context mein samjhate hain. Is se yeh nateeja nahi nikalta ke har shakhs ko har subject ka specialist banna farz hai. Farz ki zimmedari ko samajhne ke liye ilm ki qisam, shakhs ki halat aur uske amal ki zaroorat ko dekhna padta hai.

2. Fard ayn aur fard kifaya ka seedha farq

SeekersGuidance ke jawab mein fard ayn us ilm ko kehte hain jo individual ko apni zimmedari sahi ada karne ke liye chahiye. Bunyadi aqeede, taharat aur namaz ki taleem iski wazeh misaalein hain. Jab kisi par roza, zakat ya hajj ki zimmedari apni sharton ke saath aati hai, to usse related zaroori masail seekhna bhi relevant ho jata hai. Isliye ilm ki zaroorat zindagi ki halat ke saath badh sakti hai; bachpan mein jo seekha tha, wahi har naye marhale ke liye kaafi ho, yeh zaroori nahi.

Misal ke taur par koi shakhs dukaan ya online business shuru kar raha ho to uske liye transaction se related halal-haram aur huqooq ka zaroori ilm aham hoga. Nikah aur family responsibilities ka marhala aaye to doosre huqooq aur duties samajhni hongi. Yeh article kisi specific contract ka fatwa nahi de raha. Maqsad sirf yeh samjhana hai ke ilm abstract collection nahi, amal se judi zimmedari bhi hai. Jis kaam mein utarne wale hain, uski bunyadi rehnumai pehle hasil karna zyada samajhdari hai.

Fard kifaya community-level responsibility ko kehte hain. Agar kaafi log zaroori kaam sambhal lein to har individual ko usi field ka expert banna lazmi nahi rehta. Deeni sciences mein specialists ke saath medicine aur engineering jaise community ki genuine needs poori karne wale fields bhi is discussion mein aate hain. Iska matlab har fashionable career automatically har student par farz ho gaya, aisa nahi. Community ki need, kaam ki usefulness aur ethical boundaries ko saath dekhna hota hai.

Yeh farq mardon aur aurton dono ke liye zaroori taleem ki ahmiyat ko samajhne mein madad karta hai. Kisi ko basic deeni ilm se mehroom rakhna is paigham ki rooh nahi. Saath hi har reader apni personal halat ka detailed hukm ek general article se nikalne ke bajaye qualified local alim ya mufti se poochh sakta hai. Achi taleem apni had pehchanti hai; har jagah specialist ban kar jawab dena uska maqsad nahi.

3. Ilm ka adab: niyyat se sawal tak

SeekersGuidance ke adab wale mazmoon mein learning ko sirf information collection nahi, apni islaah ka safar samjha gaya hai. Niyyat ko durust karna iska aaghaz hai. Kya main seekh kar sahi amal karna chahta hoon, ya sirf behas jeetna aur logon ko neecha dikhana? Yeh sawal roz poochha ja sakta hai. Social media par ek point se doosre ko chup kara dena kabhi impressive lagta hai, lekin ilm ka akhlaqi maqsad is se kaafi bada hai.

Teacher ka ehtiram, tawajjoh se sunna aur samajh na aaye to adab se sawal karna practical aadatein hain. Ehtiram ka matlab yeh nahi ke kabhi clarification hi na maangi jaye. Balki sahi sawal learning ko mazboot karta hai. Apni notebook mein masla, uska context aur teacher ki wazahat saath likhna sirf ek catchy sentence save karne se behtar hai. Kisi aur ko baat batate waqt bhi yeh wazeh rakhein ke aapne kya samjha aur kis jagah abhi yaqeen nahi.

Ilm mein sabr bhi chahiye. Bunyad mazboot kiye baghair seedha advanced discussions mein ghusna confusion badha sakta hai. Jaise arithmetic samjhe baghair algebra ki har line mushkil lagti hai, waise deeni sciences mein bhi vocabulary aur principles ki tarteeb hoti hai. Mukhtasar clips ya isolated quotes useful reminder ho sakte hain, magar poori structured taleem ka replacement nahi. Consistent chhota lesson, revision aur amal aksar be-tarteeb bahut saari videos se zyada faida dete hain.

4. Ilm ko roz ki zindagi mein laayein

Ek simple learning routine ban sakta hai: apni zaroorat ka topic chunein, bharosemand teacher ya kitab se seekhein, jo samajh na aaye note karein, phir us par amal ki koshish karein. Agar naya ilm akhlaq ko behtar nahi kar raha, doosron ke huqooq ki fikr nahi badha raha aur apni ghalti maan-ne ki gunjaish nahi chhor raha, to apne rawayye par dobara sochna useful hai. Ilm ka husn sirf zubaan ki tezi nahi, zimmedari aur inkisari mein bhi nazar aata hai.

Online koi deeni quote share karne se pehle source check karein. Har khoobsurat jumla hadith nahi hota aur har viral reference durust nahi. Agar exact source verify na ho to use yaqini nisbat ke saath forward na karein. Yeh ehtiyaat ilm ki izzat ka hissa hai, uski raah mein rukawat nahi. Seekhna, amal karna aur jo na maloom ho use imaandari se na maloom kehna — isi rawayye se ghar aur community mein sehatmand taleemi mahaul banta hai.
