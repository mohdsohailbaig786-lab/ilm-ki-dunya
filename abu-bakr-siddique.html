Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique Radiyallahu Anhu: sidq aur rafaqat

27 November 2026 · 2 min padhai

Yeh Ahle Sunnat wal Jamaat ke aam taarrufi daire mein likha gaya ibtidai draft hai. Tashheer se pehle Sunni aalim ki jaanch baqi hai. Yeh fatwa ya tafseeli deeni rehnumai ka badal nahi.

Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique Radiyallahu Anhu Islam ke pehle khalifa aur Rasoolullah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke azeez sahabi hain. Ahle Sunnat wal Jamaat unka zikr muhabbat aur adab se karte hain.

Makkah se Madinah tak

Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique Radiyallahu Anhu taqreeban 573 CE mein Makkah Mukarramah mein paida hue. Ibtidai Islam qabool karne walon mein shamil the. Hijrat ke safar mein Rasoolullah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke saathi bane. Quran 9:40 mein ghar ke do saathiyon ka zikr hai; Sahih al-Bukhari 3653 mein Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique Radiyallahu Anhu ki ghar wali riwayat is rafaqat ko wazeh karti hai. Yahan Allah Taala ki madad aur hifazat ka mafhoom hai.

Khilafat aur zimmedari

632 se 634 CE tak Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique Radiyallahu Anhu ne pehle khalifa ki zimmedari sambhali. Markaz Madinah Munawwarah tha. Mushkil daur mein jamaat ki yakjahati aur deeni zimmedariyon ko qayam rakhna unki khidmat ka aham pehlu hai. Is mukhtasar mazmoon ka maqsad siyasi tanazaat ya tafseeli jangon ki bahas nahi.

Quran ki jama ka aham marhala

Sahih al-Bukhari 4986 mein Yamamah ke baad Quran ke likhe hue ajza ko jama karne ki zimmedari ka zikr aata hai. Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique Radiyallahu Anhu ke ahd mein yeh aham kaam hua. Quran us se pehle bhi padha, yaad kiya aur likha jata tha; jama karne ka matlab naya Quran banana nahi. 634 CE mein Madinah Munawwarah mein unki wafat hui. Unki seerat se sachchai, wafadari aur zimmedari ki qadr seekhein.

Aaj ka sawal: Kisi amanat ko mehfooz rakhne mein sachchai ke saath ehtiyat kyun zaroori hai?

Apna ilm test karo

Pehle khud sochein, phir jawab khol kar jaanchein.

1. Pehle khalifa kaun hain?
Jawab dekhein

Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique Radiyallahu Anhu.

2. Ghar ki rafaqat ka hadith hawala?
Jawab dekhein

Sahih al-Bukhari 3653.

3. Khilafat ka daur?
Jawab dekhein

632 se 634 CE.
