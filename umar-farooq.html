Hazrat Umar Farooq Radiyallahu Anhu: adl aur zimmedari

27 November 2026 · 2 min padhai

Yeh Ahle Sunnat wal Jamaat ke aam taarrufi daire mein likha gaya ibtidai draft hai. Tashheer se pehle Sunni aalim ki jaanch baqi hai. Yeh fatwa ya tafseeli deeni rehnumai ka badal nahi.

Hazrat Umar Farooq Radiyallahu Anhu doosre khalifa aur Rasoolullah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke jaleel-ul-qadr sahabi hain. Unki zindagi adl aur awami zimmedari par ghaur karne ka mauqa deti hai.

Zamana aur markaz

Hazrat Umar Farooq Radiyallahu Anhu Makkah Mukarramah mein paida hue; wiladat ke exact saal ke bare mein tareekhi andaaze mukhtalif hain. Unki khilafat 634 se 644 CE tak rahi aur markaz Madinah Munawwarah tha. 644 CE mein Madinah Munawwarah mein shahadat hui. Woh Khulfa-e-Rashideen mein doosre khalifa hain.

Nizam aur awam ki zaroorat

Hazrat Umar Farooq Radiyallahu Anhu ke ahd mein hukoomati intizam, diwan ke records aur qaza ke nizam ko taraqqi mili. Hijri san ki sarkari tarteeb bhi unke ahd se mansoob hai; saalon ki ginti Hijrat se ki gayi. Yani Hijrat ka waqia pehle hua, calendar ki intizami tarteeb baad mein hui. Dono ko ek hi saal ka waqia na samjhein.

Adl ka matlab

Adl sirf mashhoor qisse sunna nahi; faisle mein haq, amanat aur jawabdehi ko ahmiyat dena hai. Quran 4:58 amanatein ahl tak pahunchane aur logon ke darmiyan insaf se faisla karne ki taleem deta hai. Yeh aam Qurani hidayat hai, kisi khaas qisse ka saboot nahi. Hazrat Umar Farooq Radiyallahu Anhu se mansoob har viral quote ko bina hawale aage na bhejein. Sahih al-Bukhari 1 ki niyyat wali riwayat bhi unhi se marwi hai.

Aaj ka sawal: School ki zimmedari ya ghar ke kaam baantte waqt insaf kaise kiya ja sakta hai?
