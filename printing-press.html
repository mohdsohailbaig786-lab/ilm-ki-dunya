Chhapakhana (Printing Press) ki Ijaad: Gutenberg aur Kitabon ka Inqilab

27 November 2026 · 4 min padhai

Sochiye, agar har kitab ka har safha haath se likhna padta, to ilm kitni mushkil se phailta! Chhapakhane ne ek hi matn ki baar-baar nakal banana aasaan kiya. Johannes Gutenberg ka naam isi badlav se juda hai. Magar is kahani ko sirf ek shakhs ki achanak ijaad samajhna durust nahi. Iske peeche purane hunar aur naye engineering hal mil kar kaam kar rahe the.

Gutenberg se pehle bhi printing thi

Britannica ke mutabiq, movable type aur kagaz China mein pehle aa chuke the. Movable type mein har harf alag tukra hota hai, jise jod kar matn banaya aur baad mein dobara istemal kiya ja sakta hai. Duniya ki sabse purani bachi hui movable metal type se chhapi kitab Jikji hai: Korea mein 1377 mein chhapi, Gutenberg Bible se 78 saal pehle. Isliye Gutenberg ko poori duniya mein printing ka pehla inventor kehna sahi nahi.

Lakri ke screw se safha kaise chhapta tha?

Britannica printing press ka Europe mein sabse purana zikr Strasbourg ke 1439 ke ek muqadme mein batata hai. Baat Gutenberg aur uske saathiyon ke liye ban rahi press ki thi. Yeh zikr milne ki tareekh hai, kisi ek din ijaad ho jane ka certificate nahi.

Press ka model medieval paper press tha, jiski jadain purani wine aur olive presses mein thin. Ek lamba handle ghumane se bhaari lakri ka screw neeche aata aur kagaz ko siyahi lage metal type par dabata. Sochiye ek mohar, lekin yahan poore safhe ke haroof tarteeb se lage hain! Britannica ke mutabiq yeh wooden design Europe ka standard 300 saal se zyada raha aur taqreeban 250 sheets prati ghanta, ek taraf, chhap sakta tha.

Sirf dabao nahi, siyahi bhi aham thi

Gutenberg ki biography ke mutabiq usne tikau, dobara istemal hone wala metal alloy type aur oil-based ink tayyar ki. Siyahi itni gaadhi honi chahiye thi ke metal se chipke, phir kagaz ya vellum par saaf utar aaye. Vellum janwaron ki khaal se bana likhne ka material hai. Yani machine, harf aur ink ka sahi mel zaroori tha.

42-line Bible ka bada qadam

Library of Congress ke mutabiq, Mainz, Germany mein taqreeban 1455 mein Gutenberg ne Latin Vulgate wali 42-line Bible mukammal ki. Yeh Western Europe mein movable metal type se chhapi pehli badi kitab thi. Taqreeban 180 copies banin: 145 kagaz par aur 35 vellum par. Britannica batata hai ke safhe double columns mein the, har safhe par 42 lines thin; kaam ke aakhri marahil mein chhe compositors ek saath type jama rahe the.

Library of Congress ke mutabiq 49 copies 20vin sadi tak bachi thin, jin mein sirf 21 mukammal thin. Aaj sirf teen mukammal vellum copies hain: Library of Congress, Paris ki Bibliothèque Nationale aur London ki British Library mein.

Kitabon se soch ka inqilab

Haath se naqal kiye manuscripts ke muqable mein sasti printed kitabon ne maloomat ka inqilab paida kiya. Library of Congress is badlav ko Renaissance, Reformation aur Scientific Revolution ko taqat dene wala batata hai. Asal karishma sirf tez chhapai nahi tha: ideas zyada logon tak pahunch sakte the.

Aaj ka sawal: Agar kitabein aasani se na chhaptin, to naye ideas logon tak kaise pahunchte?
