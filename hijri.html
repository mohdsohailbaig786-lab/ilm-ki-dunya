Islamic Calendar

Aaj ki hisabi Hijri tareekh

Zaroori wazahat — chand ki tasdeeq

Ye hisaab calculation (hisabi tareekh) par based hai; asal Islamic tareekh chand dikhne (ruyat-e-hilal) par mukarrar hoti hai. Mahine ke aghaz aur khatam ke liye apni local hilal committee ki tasdeeq zaroori hai. Is calendar ko roza, Eid ya deeni aamaal ki aakhri tasdeeq na samjhein.

Algorithm: 30 saala tabular Islamic civil calendar (Friday epoch, 16 July 622 Julian). Ye Saudi Umm al-Qura ya live chand ki report nahin. Aaj ki tareekh device ke local din se li gayi hai; Maghrib par khud nahin badalti.

Tareekh badlein

Upar Hijri din, neeche Gregorian din aur mahina. Hafte ka aghaz Itwaar se.

Aane wale aham Islami din

Ye tamam tareekhein bhi hisabi hain; ruyat-e-hilal ki tasdeeq zaroor karein.
