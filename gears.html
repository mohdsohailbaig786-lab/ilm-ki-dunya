Gears kaise kaam karte hain? Gear ratio ka raaz

27 November 2026 · 2 min padhai

Cycle par chadhai chadhte waqt pedal halka ya bhaari kyun lagta hai? Gears speed aur torque ke darmiyan tabadla karte hain; muft energy paida nahi karte.

Daant se daant tak

Do toothed wheels jab ek doosre mein mesh karte hain to ek ki rotation doosre tak pahunchti hai. Bahar se milne wale do simple gears ulte rukh mein ghoomte hain. Driver input gear hota hai; driven output gear. Daant phisalne ke bajaye muqarrar nisbat se movement dete hain.

Ratio ka seedha hisaab

Is article mein gear ratio = driven ke daant / driver ke daant. Agar 10-daant driver, 30-daant driven ko ghumaye to ratio 3:1 hai. Driver ke teen chakkaron par output ka ek chakkar hoga. Ideal halat mein output torque taqreeban teen guna, speed ek-tihai hogi. Asal mein friction ki wajah se kuch energy heat mein chali jati hai.

Cycle ki misaal

Cycle mein chain do sprockets ko jorti hai; woh seedhe meshing gears nahi. Bada rear sprocket chhote front ke muqable mein wheel ko har pedal turn par kam ghumata hai, is liye chadhai aasaan lagti hai. Dono sprockets chain se aam tor par ek hi rotational direction mein ghoomte hain. Har baar ratio samajhte waqt input aur output saaf pehchanein. Chalti chain ya gears mein ungli, kapra ya auzar na daalein.

Aaj ka sawal: Chadhai par aasaani ke badle har pedal turn par kitna fasla tay hota hai?

Apna ilm test karo

Pehle khud sochein, phir jawab khol kar jaanchein.

1. Ratio ka yahan formula?
Jawab dekhein

Driven teeth / driver teeth.

2. 10 se 30 teeth mein output ki speed?
Jawab dekhein

Input ki ek-tihai.

3. Kya gears energy paida karte hain?
Jawab dekhein

Nahin; speed aur torque ka tabadla karte hain.
