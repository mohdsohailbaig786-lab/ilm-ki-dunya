Suraj kitna bada hai? Dimagh mein na aane wala jawab

24 November 2026

Subah ki chai ke saath nazar aane wala Suraj ek chhoti chamakti disk lagta hai. Magar yeh chhotapan uska size nahi, hamari doori ka asar hai. NASA ke Sun Facts ke mutabiq uska diameter taqreeban 14 lakh kilometre hai. Diameter yaani gole ke ek kinaare se centre ke raaste doosre kinaare tak ki doori. Is ek number ko samajhne ke liye pehle kilometre se bahar nikal kar ek chhota sa model banate hain. Phir dekhenge ke is bade taare ke andar kya chal raha hai, aur iska hamari rozmarra zindagi se kitna gehra rishta hai.

1. Zameen ko paas rakh kar dekhein

Suraj ka diameter Zameen ke diameter se lagbhag 109 guna hai. Sochiye ek line mein qareeban 109 Zameenein rakh di jayein: tab ja kar Suraj ki chaudai ka andaaza banega. Lekin chaudai aur andar ki jagah ek cheez nahi. NASA ke mutabiq Suraj ke volume mein qareeban 13 lakh Zameenon jitni jagah aa sakti hai. Yeh geometric comparison hai, asal mein sakht gole bharne ka packing experiment nahi. Jab kisi gole ka size har direction mein badhta hai, uska volume sirf utne guna nahi, bahut zyada badhta hai.

Ab mass dekhein, yaani usmein maujood matter ki miqdaar. Suraj ka mass Zameen se 3 lakh 30 hazaar guna se bhi zyada hai. Volume wala 13 lakh aur mass wala 3.3 lakh alag numbers kyun? Kyunki dono ki average density ek jaisi nahi. Zameen mein pathar aur dhaatu ka bada hissa hai; Suraj zyada tar hydrogen aur helium se bana hai. Bada hona aur har litre mein zyada bhaari hona alag baatein hain. Isi liye kisi article mein sirf “itni Zameenein aa jayengi” sun kar poochhna chahiye: baat chaudai ki hai, volume ki ya mass ki?

NASA ke hisaab se solar system ke total mass ka qareeban 99.8 percent Suraj mein hai. Baqi planets, moons aur doosri cheezein mil kar bacha hua chhota hissa banti hain. Yahan size se zyada mass aham hai: Suraj ki gravity solar system ke orbital nizaam mein markazi kirdar ada karti hai. Planets usmein gir kyun nahi jaate? Unki aage ki harkat aur gravity mil kar unke orbital raaste banati hain; yeh kisi nazar na aane wali sakht rassi se bandhe nahi hain.

2. Ek metre ka Suraj, sau metre se aage Zameen

Zameen aur Suraj ki average doori qareeban 15 crore kilometre hai. Average ka lafz zaroori hai, kyunki hamara orbit bilkul perfect circle nahi. Is distance ko astronomy mein ek astronomical unit, ya AU, ka benchmark mana jata hai. Ab model mein Suraj ka diameter sirf ek metre kar dein. Upar ke rounded numbers ko divide karne se Zameen ka diameter ek centimetre se thoda kam aur uski average doori qareeban 107 metre niklegi. Yeh hamara calculated scale model hai, NASA ka alag measured number nahi.

Ek metre ka chamakta gola colony ke gate par rakhein aur chhota sa daana football ground ke doosre sire ke aas paas le jayein. Beech mein zyada tar khaali jagah hogi. School ke solar system charts mein planets ko paas paas banana drawing ki majboori hai, asal scale nahi. Agar sizes aur distances dono ko ek hi scale par dikhaya jaye to poora nizaam notebook ke ek panne mein readable rehna mushkil ho jayega. Space ki pehli hairat yahi hai: bade objects ke darmiyan us se bhi zyada badi dooriyan hoti hain.

Suraj ki roshni ko Zameen tak aane mein qareeban aath minute lagte hain. Iska matlab hum use bilkul isi lamhe jaisa nahi, kuch minute pehle jaisa dekhte hain. Light bahut tez hai, phir bhi kainaat ki dooriyan uske liye bhi safar ban jati hain. Yeh vacuum mein light ke safar ki baat hai. Suraj ke andar energy ka bahar ki taraf nikalna mukhtalif, bahut zyada uljha hua process hai; dono ko mila kar yeh nahi kehna chahiye ke centre ki energy bhi sirf aath minute mein hamare ghar aa gayi.

3. Aag ka chulha nahi, fusion ka taara

Suraj qareeban 4.6 arab saal pehle gas aur dust ke ek bade cloud se bana. Roz ke chulhe mein fuel oxygen ke saath jalta hai; Suraj ka bunyadi energy source aisi chemical aag nahi. Uske core mein nuclear fusion ke zariye hydrogen se helium banti hai aur energy release hoti hai. NASA core ka temperature qareeban 1.5 crore degree Celsius batata hai. Itni extreme conditions mein matter plasma hota hai: aam neutral gas se mukhtalif, jisme charged particles ka kirdar aham hota hai.

Jis roshan satah ko hum tasveer mein dekhte hain use photosphere kehte hain. Iska temperature qareeban 5,500 degree Celsius hai, lekin yeh Zameen jaisi solid zameen nahi jahan spacecraft utar kar khara ho sake. Andar core, radiative zone aur convection zone hain. Bahar chromosphere aur corona jaise atmospheric layers hain. Corona ka photosphere se kaafi zyada garam hona solar physics ka dilchasp sawal hai. Is se ek achha lesson milta hai: centre se door jaate hi temperature har layer mein seedhi line mein kam hota rahe, aisa zaroori nahi.

Suraj ka ghoomna bhi cricket ball jaisa ek hi rate par nahi. NASA equator ke paas rotation qareeban 25 Earth days aur poles ke paas qareeban 36 days batata hai. Yeh differential rotation hai. Magnetic fields is badalte, hilte plasma ke saath interact karti hain. Sunspots, solar flares aur doosri activity ka rishta isi magnetic duniya se hai. Sunspots comparatively thande ilaqe hain; unhe kala dikhna is baat ka saboot nahi ke woh ghar ke kamre ki tarah thande hain.

4. Ek badalta hua padosi

NASA Space Place ke mutabiq solar activity ka cycle aam taur par qareeban 11 saal ka hota hai. Solar minimum mein sunspots kam aur maximum mein zyada ho sakte hain. Yeh exact calendar alarm nahi, natural cycle ka andaaza hai. Suraj ki magnetic polarity bhi flip hoti hai; poori magnetic configuration ko wapas aane mein qareeban do aise cycles lagte hain. Space weather satellites, radio communication aur power infrastructure ko affect kar sakta hai. Hamara technically connected zamana taare ki activity se bilkul alag duniya nahi.

Suraj khud bhi solar system ko saath le kar Milky Way ke centre ke gird safar karta hai. NASA ek orbit ke liye qareeban 23 crore saal ka andaaza deta hai. Aage kai arab saal mein iski evolution red giant aur aakhir white dwarf ki taraf hogi; yeh kal subah ka khatra nahi. Filhaal sabse practical baat yaad rakhein: Suraj ko seedha dekhna aankhon ko nuqsan pahuncha sakta hai. Uska ilm safe images aur proper solar observing equipment se hasil karein, naked-eye tajrube se nahi.
