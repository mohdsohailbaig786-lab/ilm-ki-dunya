Fatwa Corner: Ala Hazrat (RA) ke chune hue fatawa

27 November 2026 · 6 min padhai

Zaroori note: Yeh mukhtasar jawabat hain; asal kitab Fatawa-e-Razvia hai. Yeh naya fatwa ya zaati surat-e-haal ka faisla nahin. Tafseel ke liye apne mustanad Sunni mufti/aalim se rabta karein.

Imam Ahmed Raza Khan (RA) ke ilm se faida uthane mein asal sawal, jawab aur hawala saath padhna zaroori hai. Yahan paanch aam masail ke muntakhab hisse Roman Urdu mein pesh hain; lafzi tarjuma nahin. Kitab ke downloads aur mukammal majmue ke liye Ala Hazrat (RA) ka free kutub khana dekhein.

1. Aqeeqa: ek janwar kitne bachon ki taraf se?

Sawal: Do ya zyada bachon ke aqeeqe mein ek bakri par sab ki niyyat kaafi hai ya alag janwar chahiye?

Jawab ka mukhtasar: Ala Hazrat (RA) ne jawab diya ke gaaye aur oont saat bachon ki taraf se kaafi hain, jabke bher aur bakri ek se zyada bachon ke liye kaafi nahin; is ki misaal qurbani ki tarah di.

Hawala: Fatawa-e-Razvia, jild 20, Bab al-Aqeeqa, masla 296. Dawat-e-Islami ka asal matn: safha 591.

2. Qurbani ki khaal: istemal aur khair ka kaam

Sawal: Qurbani ki khaal kis ko di ja sakti hai, aur kya use masjid ke kaam mein la sakte hain? (Taveel sawal ka muntakhab hissa.)

Jawab ka mukhtasar: Ala Hazrat (RA) ne farmaya ke qurbani Allah ke liye khoon bahane ka naam hai aur wajib itne se ada ho jata hai. Gosht aur khaal ko apne istemal mein lana, zaroorat ke liye rakhna, ya sawab ke kaam mein lagana bayan farmaya; sawal mein diye gaye masjid ke kaam bhi is mein shamil hain. Saath wazeh kiya ke ajza-e-qurbani ko bech kar unke daam apne kaam mein lana mana hai. Yeh sirf jawab ka muntakhab hissa hai; khaal ke doosre masarif ki tafseel asal jawab mein dekhein.

Hawala: Fatawa-e-Razvia, jild 20, Kitab al-Udhiyah, masla 255. Dawat-e-Islami ka asal matn: safha 488.

3. Zakaat: zevar mein izafa ho to?

Sawal: Ek shakhs apne zevar ki zakaat deta hai; zevar barh jaye to zakaat kis hisaab se barhegi?

Jawab ka mukhtasar: Ala Hazrat (RA) ne is jawab mein hajat-e-asliya se farigh sone ke nisab par qamri saal guzarne ke baad chaliswan hissa bayan farmaya. Sone ka nisab saadhe saat (7.5) tole aur chandi ka saadhe bawan (52.5) tole likha. Nisab se zyada miqdar ke liye Imam-e-Azam (RA) ke qaul par har poora panchwan hissa (khums-e-nisab) barhne par us izafe ka chaliswan hissa bayan kiya, aur do mukammal khums ke darmiyan izafa maaf bataya. Agle safhe par yeh bhi samjhaya ke saal ke darmiyan usi jins ka naya maal milne par use asal nisab ke saath milaya jata hai, bashart-e-ke is se ek maal par ek saal mein do baar zakaat lazim na aaye. Yeh us khaas jawab ka khulasa hai, aaj ki raqam nikalne ka calculator nahin; apne tamam maal aur qarz ki tafseel mufti ko batayein.

Hawala: Fatawa-e-Razvia, jild 10, Kitab al-Zakat, masla thaniya; safha 88–89. Dawat-e-Islami ka asal matn: safha 88 · Safha 89.

4. Roza: khatti dakar ka sawal

Sawal: Sehri mein zyada kha lene se subah khatti dakarein aayein to kya roza nahin hota? (Masla 223 ka pehla hissa.)

Jawab ka mukhtasar: Ala Hazrat (RA) ne saaf jawab diya ke khatti dakar se roza nahin jata; yeh baat ke is se roza nahin hota kisi kitab mein nahin likhi. Yahan sirf isi sawal ke is hisse ka khulasa hai, qay ya doosri alag suraton ka hukm nahin diya ja raha.

Hawala: Fatawa-e-Razvia, jild 10, Mufsidat-e-Sawm, masla 223 ka pehla hissa. Dawat-e-Islami ka asal matn: safha 492.

5. Iftar: kis cheez se roza kholna masnoon hai?

Sawal: Roza kis cheez se iftar karna masnoon hai? (Masail 269–270 mein pehla sawal.)

Jawab ka mukhtasar: Ala Hazrat (RA) ne jawab diya: tar khajoor, woh na ho to khushk khajoor, aur woh bhi na ho to paani. Is jawab mein Nabi-e-Karim ﷺ ke namaz se pehle is tarah iftar farmane ki riwayat naql ki. Yeh sirf pehle sawal ka khulasa hai.

Hawala: Fatawa-e-Razvia, jild 10, Sehar wa Iftar ka bayan, masail 269–270 ka pehla sawal; sawal safha 637, jawab 638. Dawat-e-Islami ka asal matn: safha 638 · Safha 637.
