Hazrat Bilal Habshi (RA) - "Ahad, Ahad"

Takleefon mein bhi Allah ki wahdaniyat par yaqeen.

Poora waqia padhein

Hazrat Bilal (RA) Habshi ghulam the aur Islam lane ke baad unke malik Umayyah bin Khalaf unhe bahut satate. Tapti hui chattaan par unhe leta kar seeti hui tiliaur patthar rakh diye jate, lekin Bilal (RA) ki zubaan sirf ek jumla bolti: "Ahad, Ahad" - matlab Allah ek hai. Unka iman hilta nahin tha. Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) ne unhe kharid kar azad karwaya. Allah ke Rasool ﷺ ne unhe Islam ka pehla Muezzin banaya, aur jannat mein bhi Allah ke Rasool ﷺ ko unke qadam ki aahat sunai di - ye Sahih Bukhari aur Muslim ki riwayat hai. Ek ghulam, jise duniya ki sabse badi takleefen di gayin, aaj poori ummat ka Muezzin bana.

Source: Sahih Bukhari o Muslim; Sirat Ibn Hisham.

Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique (RA) - Ghar-e-Saur Ka Saathi

Hijrat ke safar mein saath aur bharosa.

Poora waqia padhein

Hijrat ki raat Allah ke Rasool ﷺ aur Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) Makkah se nikle aur Ghar-e-Saur (cave) mein chhup gaye. Abu Bakr (RA) ne pehle khud andar jaa kar har kone check kiya, phir Aaqa ﷺ ko andar aane diya. Mushrikeen cave ke munh tak aa gaye. Abu Bakr (RA) ne ghabrahat se kaha: "Ya Rasoolallah, agar koi andhere ko dekh le to hamen pakad lega." Aaqa ﷺ ne farmaya: "Ghabraao mat, Allah hamare saath hai." Yehi qaul Quran mein Surah Taubah aayat 40 mein aaya. Abu Bakr (RA) ki is sachai ki gawahi mein unhe "Siddique" kaha gaya - jo sach ko tasdeeq karne wala ho.

Source: Quran (Surah Taubah 9:40); Sahih Bukhari o Muslim.

Hazrat Umar Farooq (RA) - Sakht Dushman Se Islam Ka Naseeb

Kalam sun kar dil badalne ka waqia.

Poora waqia padhein

Hazrat Umar (RA) shuru mein Islam ke sakht dushman the. Ek din talwaar bandh kar Allah ke Rasool ﷺ ko maarne nikle. Raaste mein pata chala ke unki behen Fatima (RA) Islam lai hain. Unke ghar pahunche to unhone apne ghar mein Surah Taha ki tilawat suni. Kalam padh kar unka dil pighal gaya. Wahi se ruk kar seedhe Allah ke Rasool ﷺ ke paas gaye aur Islam qubool kar liya. Unke Islam lane se Muslims ko bahut taqat mili - ab log Khana-e-Kaaba ke paas khul kar namaz padhne lage. Allah ne unki hidayat ke baad unhe Farooq (haq o baat mein faisla karne wala) ka laqab diya.

Source: Sirat Ibn Hisham; Sahih Bukhari (Umar ke Islam ka zikr).

Hazrat Usman Ghani (RA) - Jayada Dana, Kam Bolna

Zaroorat ke waqt khule dil se madad.

Poora waqia padhein

Ghazwa-e-Tabuk ke waqt Muslims ke paas sarkeejanaur khane ka saaman bahut kam tha. Allah ke Rasool ﷺ ne madad ka isida kiya. Hazrat Usman (RA) ne hazaron dinar laye aur poore lashkar ka bada hissa khud saaman seooros kiya - riwayat mein aata hai ke unhonne apne dasta ka bahut bada hissa wahan khud kharch kiya. Aaqa ﷺ ne unki qurbani dekh kar dua farmayi. Isi tarah pehle unhone Makkah mein jakat ke waqt bhi bade bade kharch kiye. Hazrat Usman (RA) bolne mein kam, dene mein sab se aage the.

Source: Sahih Bukhari (Ghazwa-e-Tabuk ke maqat); Ibn Sa'd.

Hazrat Mus'ab bin Umair (RA) - Madinah Ka Pehla Ustaad

Aish se qurbani tak, deen sikhane ka safar.

Poora waqia padhein

Hazrat Mus'ab (RA) Makkah ke ameeron mein se the - kapde, khushboo, aish o ishrat sab kuch tha. Islam lene ke baad unki halat badal gayi, lekin iman mazboot ho gaya. Hijrat se pehle Allah ke Rasool ﷺ ne unhe Madinah ka pehla Islam teacher bana kar bheja. Wahan unhone naram andaz se logon ko Allah ka deen samjhaya, aur Aqsa ke bade sardar - Hazrat Sa'd bin Mu'adh (RA) aur Hazrat Usaid bin Hudair (RA) - Islam laye. Uhud ke maidan mein unhe shahadat mili. Jo ek shaista nawab tha, wo Allah ke deen ke liye sab kuch luta gaya.

Source: Sirat Ibn Hisham; Sahih Bukhari (riwayat ke hawale ke saath).
