Safar aur ilm: Muslim civilisation mein science ka safar

24 November 2026

Ilm ka safar aksar kitaab ke safar se juda hota hai: ek zubaan se doosri zubaan, ek shehar se doosre shehar, phir ek naye sawal tak. Medieval Muslim civilisations ki scientific history isi movement ki dilchasp misaal hai. Baghdad, translations, mathematics, astronomy aur medicine ke naam aate hain, magar isko “saara science ek hi jagah invent ho gaya” wali kahani banana sahi nahi. Scholars ne Greek, Indian, Persian aur doosri traditions se seekha, un par kaam kiya aur naye contributions kiye. Respectful history tareef ke saath accuracy bhi chahti hai.

1. Baghdad aur tarjume ki duniya

University of St Andrews ke MacTutor archive ke mutabiq al-Khwarizmi qareeban 780 se 850 CE ke darmiyan zinda rahe. Unka scholarly kaam Baghdad mein al-Mamun ke daur aur House of Wisdom ke intellectual environment se related tha. Greek scientific writings ke translations aur mathematics aur astronomy par research is milieu ka aham hissa the. Banu Musa jaise scholars bhi isi broader scholarly duniya mein kaam kar rahe the. Patronage, manuscripts aur trained readers mil kar research ko possible banate the.

House of Wisdom ko aaj ke ek modern university campus ki exact copy samajhna ehtiyaat ke khilaf hai. Historical institutions ke functions aur unke baare mein surviving evidence alag nature ke hote hain. Har famous medieval scientist ko ek hi building ka employee bana dena bhi ghalat hai. Phir bhi Baghdad ka translation aur scholarship centre ke taur par role important tha. Kitaab ko doosri zubaan mein laana sirf words badalna nahi: technical terminology samajhna aur difficult arguments ko dobara check karna bhi mehnat hai.

Is intellectual network mein mukhtalif zubaanon aur backgrounds ke logon ka kirdar tha. “Muslim civilisation” ek broad historical setting ka naam hai, har contributor ki identical personal identity ka daawa nahi. Is distinction se contribution chhota nahi hota; cooperation ki scale samajh aati hai. Ilm jab doosron se seekhne ko beizzati nahi samajhta, tab naye sawal aur naye methods paida ho sakte hain.

2. Al-Khwarizmi: algebra aur practical hisaab

Al-Khwarizmi ke algebra par kaam ka taalluq linear aur quadratic equations ke systematic treatment se tha. Al-jabr wal-muqabala ke title ka al-jabr aaj ke “algebra” lafz ki history se juda hai. Unke naam ki Latin forms se “algorithm” ka lafz nikla. Iska matlab yeh nahi ke unhone har possible algorithm ya poori mathematics akelay invent ki. Sahi tareef yeh hai ke unke influential works ne calculation aur problem-solving ko organise aur transmit karne mein bada role ada kiya.

Us zamane mein maths sirf abstract puzzle nahi: inheritance, trade aur land measurement jaise practical sawal bhi aham the. Aaj hum equation symbols se likhte hain, magar historical works mein prose aur geometric reasoning ka apna role tha. Modern notation ko seedha purani manuscript par chipka dena history ko distort kar sakta hai. Ek useful lesson yeh hai ke method ki taqat aksar uski repeatability mein hoti hai: sirf ek problem ka jawab nahi, us type ke sawalon ko hal karne ka tareeqa.

Indian numerals aur reckoning ke transmission mein bhi al-Khwarizmi ka naam important hai. Yahan ilm ek chain hai: pehle se maujood ideas ko samajhna, unhe adapt karna aur aage le jana. Civilisations ko ek doosre se bilkul sealed boxes samajhna mathematics ki actual history ko samajhne mein rukawat banta hai.

3. Ibn al-Haytham aur dekhne ka sawal

MacTutor Ibn al-Haytham ki zindagi qareeban 965–1039 CE batata hai. Optics mein unka kaam is sawal se juda tha ke hum dekhte kaise hain. Vision ko aankh se cheezon ki taraf kuch nikalne ke simple model ke bajaye, objects se aankh tak aane wali light se samajhna ek aham direction tha. Light, geometry aur observation ko saath use karna unke work ki khas ahmiyat hai.

Unhe akela “scientific method ka inventor” keh dena attractive headline hai, magar history zyada complex hai. Observation, reasoning aur testing ki traditions kai jagahon par evolve hui. Unka contribution isi broad story mein apni genuine jagah rakhta hai. Research ki achhi misaal yeh hai ke established explanation ko sirf authority ki wajah se accept na kiya jaye; uske predictions aur evidence ko examine bhi kiya jaye.

4. Medicine ki kitaab ka centuries-long safar

US National Library of Medicine Ibn Sina ke Canon of Medicine ko ek massive medical encyclopaedia ke taur par describe karti hai. Unki wafat 1037 mein hui. NLM ke account mein kitab ka kaam Gurgan se shuru, Rayy aur phir Hamadan tak ke safar se juda hai. Is tarah ek influential work bhi ek din ki dictation nahi, lambi scholarly mehnat ka nateeja tha.

Canon ki Gerard of Cremona wali Latin translation twelfth century mein widely read hui. NLM ke mutabiq 1500 aur 1674 ke darmiyan iske hisson ya poori kitab ke qareeban 60 editions publish hue. Yeh influence ka concrete example hai. Magar medieval textbook ki historical importance ka matlab uski har treatment ko aaj ki medical advice samajhna nahi. Modern medicine ko current clinical evidence se judge karna hota hai, historical prestige se nahi.

Is poore safar ka paigham na superiority contest hai na purane naam yaad karne ki race. Kitaabon ko preserve karna, teachers se seekhna, translations karna aur evidence ke saath naye sawal poochhna communities ko aage badhata hai. Ilm ki izzat ka ek hissa yeh bhi hai ke doosron ke contribution ko acknowledge karein aur apni pasand ki history mein bhi exaggeration se bachhein.
