Ibn al-Haytham: roshni, nazar aur tajurba

27 November 2026 · 2 min padhai

Kya aankh se kirnein nikal kar cheezon ko dekhti hain, ya roshni aankh tak aati hai? Is sawal ki jaanch optics ki tareekh mein bohot aham thi.

Basra se Misr tak

Ibn al-Haytham taqreeban 965 CE mein Basra mein paida hue aur taqreeban 1040 CE mein Cairo mein wafat pai. Muslim mathematician aur optics ke scholar the. Latin kitaabon mein Alhazen ke naam se pehchane gaye.

Kitab al-Manazir

Unki Kitab al-Manazir ne nazar ko cheezon se aankh tak aane wali roshni ke zariye samjhaya. Unhon ne reflection, refraction aur aankh ki banawat par bahas ki. Andhere kamre mein chhote suraakh se aati roshni ka mutalaa, seedhi lakeer mein roshni ke safar ko samajhne mein madad deta hai. Yeh modern camera ki poori ijaad ka dawa nahi.

Saboot ko azmaana

Unhon ne mathematical daleel ko observation aur experiments se jora. Isi liye unka naam early experimental scientific method ki tareekh mein aata hai. Scientific method ek lambi mushtarka taraqqi hai; ise ek shakhs ka ek din mein banaya hua formula kehna theek nahi. Kitab al-Manazir ke Latin tarjume ne European optics ko mutasir kiya, jahan baad ke scholars ne is kaam par mazeed bahas aur research ki.

Aaj ka sawal: Agar roshni aankh mein na pahunche to andhere kamre mein cheez kyun nazar nahi aati?

Apna ilm test karo

Pehle khud sochein, phir jawab khol kar jaanchein.

1. Kitab al-Manazir ka mauzu?
Jawab dekhein

Optics aur nazar.

2. Dekhne mein roshni kis taraf aati hai?
Jawab dekhein

Cheez se aankh tak.

3. Kya scientific method sirf ek shakhs ki ijaad hai?
Jawab dekhein

Nahin; kai zamano ke scholars ne is mein hissa liya.
