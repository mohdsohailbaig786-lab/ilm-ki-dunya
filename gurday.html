Gurday kaise kaam karte hain? Jism ke khamosh chemists

28 November 2026 · 4 min padhai

Sochiye, ghar ka water filter sirf gandagi rokta hai. Lekin aapke gurday, ya kidneys, is se kaafi zyada samajhdar nizaam ka hissa hain: woh khoon se fazool madday nikalte hain, zaroori cheezein sambhalte hain aur jism ka andarooni balance banaye rakhte hain. National Kidney Foundation (NKF) inhein jism ke master chemists kehta hai. Aaiye dekhein ke yeh khamosh laboratory kaise chalti hai.

Chhote size mein bada kaam

NKF ke mutabiq, zyada tar log do gurdon ke saath paida hote hain. Har ek taqreeban adult ki mutthi jitna hota hai aur reedh ki haddi ke dono taraf, pasliyon ke neeche maujood hota hai. Itne chhote organs sirf peshab banane ki machine nahi: yeh waste aur extra fluid nikalne, blood pressure sambhalne, red blood cells banne mein madad aur haddiyon ko sehatmand rakhne mein hissa lete hain.

Nephron: bareek filter aur naali ki jodi

Har gurday mein taqreeban das lakh filtering units, jinhein nephrons kehte hain, hote hain. Har nephron mein khoon ki chhoti naaliyon ka filtering guchcha, glomerulus, ek tubule se juda hota hai. Tubule ko bareek naali samajh lijiye. Khoon pehle filter hota hai; phir nikla hua fluid is naali se guzarta hai.

Yahin asal selection hoti hai. Jism ki zaroorat ke mutabiq paani aur chemicals filtered fluid mein shamil ya us se wapas liye jaate hain. Yani filter se guzarna har cheez ke jism se bahar chale jaane ka naam nahi! Zaroori cheezon ko bachana bhi safai jitna aham hai.

200 quarts filter, magar 2 quarts peshab?

NKF ke diye hue taqreebi hisaab mein gurday har 24 ghante khoon se 200 quarts fluid filter karte hain. Is mein se lagbhag 198 quarts jism mein reh jaate hain aur kareeban 2 quarts peshab ki soorat mein nikalte hain. In figures ko samajhne ke liye sochiye: 200 mein se sirf 2, yani taqreeban 1 feesad, aakhri output hai.

Iska matlab yeh nahi ke roz itna naya khoon ban raha hai, ya itna paani peena chahiye. Yeh filtering ka jama hisaab hai, peene ki hidayat nahi. Peshab ureters naam ki naaliyon se bladder, yani masane, tak pahunchta hai. Wahan jama hone ke baad urethra ke raaste bahar aata hai. Ureter aur urethra ke naam milte-julte hain, magar raaste alag hain.

Sirf safai nahi, chemical balance bhi

NKF samjhata hai ke gurday namak, potassium aur acid ka balance bhi sambhalte hain. Isliye unka kaam kisi chhalni jaisa seedha nahi. Unki kharabi doosre organs par bhi asar daal sakti hai. Diabetes aur high blood pressure kidney disease ki aham wajahen hain; inki dekhbhaal kidney health se bhi judi hai.

Sehat ka haal kaise maloom ho?

NKF do tests batata hai: khoon se eGFR, jo filtering function ka andaza deta hai, aur peshab se uACR, jo albumin protein aur creatinine ka ratio dekhta hai. Dono mil kar tasveer zyada saaf karte hain. Apne results aur testing ki zaroorat healthcare professional se samjhein; yeh mazmoon aam taleemi maloomat hai, personal medical advice nahi.

Aaj ka sawal: Agar gurday filtered paani ko wapas na bachate, to jism ka balance kaise badalta?
