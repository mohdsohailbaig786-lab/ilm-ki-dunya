Water pump: paani upar kaise aata hai?

27 November 2026 · 2 min padhai

Ghar ki tanki ho ya borewell, pump paani ko energy de kar uska flow aur pressure badhata hai. Har pump ka tareeqa ek jaisa nahi.

Centrifugal pump

Motor impeller ko ghumati hai. Ghoomte blades paani ko energy dete hain; casing aur flow passages is movement ke ek hisse ko pressure mein badalne mein madad karte hain. Inlet aur outlet ke pressure ka farq paani ke flow ko mumkin banata hai. Yeh paani ko bina energy ke upar kheenchne ka jadoo nahi.

Borewell aur suction ki had

Surface pump jab inlet pressure kam karta hai to source ki satah par atmospheric pressure paani ko pipe mein dhakelta hai. Sea level par water column ki theoretical suction limit taqreeban 10 metre hai; practical limit losses aur conditions ki wajah se kam hoti hai. Gehri borewell mein submersible pump paani ke andar lag kar use upar push karta hai; isi liye gehrai ko sirf lambi suction pipe se hal nahi kiya ja sakta.

Flow, head aur dekhbhal

Head ko energy per unit weight ki height ke taur par samjhein. Zyada vertical lift aur lambi ya tang pipe ka friction pump ke required head ko badhata hai. Har pump har flow par kaam nahi karta: pump curve aur system ki zaroorat milni chahiye. Kai centrifugal pumps ko priming chahiye aur dry run nuksan de sakta hai. Paani ke paas electric wiring ko na chhedein; installation qualified technician kare.

Aaj ka sawal: Tanki ko aur uncha kar dene se usi pump ka flow kyun kam ho sakta hai?

Apna ilm test karo

Pehle khud sochein, phir jawab khol kar jaanchein.

1. Centrifugal pump mein kya ghoomta hai?
Jawab dekhein

Impeller.

2. Deep borewell mein aam hal?
Jawab dekhein

Paani mein laga submersible pump.

3. Head badhne se flow hamesha wahi rehta hai?
Jawab dekhein

Nahin; nateeja pump curve aur system par depend karta hai.
