Seerat-un-Nabi: Rasoolullah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki hayat ka taarruf

27 November 2026 · 2 min padhai

Yeh Ahle Sunnat wal Jamaat ke aam taarrufi daire mein likha gaya ibtidai draft hai. Tashheer se pehle Sunni aalim ki jaanch baqi hai. Yeh fatwa ya tafseeli deeni rehnumai ka badal nahi.

Rasoolullah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki seerat padhna akhlaq, amanat aur rehmat ki taleem samajhne ka zariya hai. Quran 33:21 mein Rasoolullah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki zindagi ko momineen ke liye behtareen namoona qarar diya gaya hai. Yeh mafhoom ka khulasa hai, lafzi tarjuma nahi.

Makkah Mukarramah ka daur

Rasoolullah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki wiladat Makkah Mukarramah mein Aam-ul-Feel mein hui, jise aam tareekhi hisaab taqreeban 570 CE se jorta hai. Chalees saal ki umr mein pehli wahi ka aghaz hua. Makki daur mein tauheed, akhirat, ibadat aur achhe akhlaq ki dawat di gayi. Mushkilat ke bawajood sabr aur sachchai ki taleem jari rahi.

Hijrat aur Madinah Munawwarah

622 CE mein Rasoolullah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ne Makkah Mukarramah se Madinah Munawwarah hijrat farmai. Wahan masjid aur musalman muashray ki bunyaadein mazboot huin. Muhajireen aur Ansar ke darmiyan bhai-chara qayam hua. Hijrat sirf jagah badalne ka naam nahi; deen par amal aur ek doosre ki madad ki tareekh bhi hai.

Akhlaq aur rehmat

Quran 21:107 mein Rasoolullah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ko tamam jahanon ke liye rehmat bana kar bhejne ka zikr hai. Quran 68:4 mein buland akhlaq ka bayan hai. In taleemat se sach bolna, wada nibhana, kamzor ka khayal rakhna aur narmi se pesh aana seekhte hain. Sahih al-Bukhari 1 ki riwayat aamaal mein niyyat ki ahmiyat batati hai.

Aakhri daur

Rasoolullah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ne 632 CE mein Hajjat-ul-Wada ada farmaya. Isi saal Madinah Munawwarah mein wisal farmaya. Yeh mukhtasar timeline hai; har waqia ki tafseel mustanad seerat ki kitaab aur ahl-e-ilm se samajhni chahiye. Ghair-mustanad qisson ko seerat ka hissa bana kar aage na phelayein.

Aaj ka sawal: Ghar ya school mein amanat aur sachchai ki taleem par aaj kaunsa amal kiya ja sakta hai?
