Mobile phone ki battery kaise kaam karti hai? Lithium-ion ki kahani

24 November 2026

Phone subah full charge tha, shaam tak battery laal ho gayi. Screen par ek percentage dikh raha hai, lekin andar chemistry aur electrical engineering ki team kaam kar rahi hai. Lithium-ion battery koi chhota petrol tank nahi jisme electricity liquid ki tarah bhar di gayi ho. Yeh chemical energy store karti hai aur controlled reactions ke zariye electrical energy deti hai. US Department of Energy ke explainers isko electrodes, electrolyte aur moving charged particles ke saath samjhate hain. Chaliye phone ko khole baghair uske andar ka safar samajhte hain.

1. Ions andar, electrons bahar

Ek cell mein do electrodes, electrolyte aur separator jaise hisse hote hain. Discharge ke waqt lithium ions negative electrode, ya anode, se positive electrode, ya cathode, ki taraf electrolyte ke zariye move karte hain. Electrons ka useful safar external circuit se hota hai. Isi flow se phone ke circuits ko energy milti hai. Separator electrodes ko direct contact se bachata hai, lekin ions ko guzarna hota hai. Agar internal short circuit ho jaye to energy bahut tezi se release ho sakti hai, jo dangerous hai.

Charging mein charger energy supply karta hai aur lithium ions ko wapas doosri taraf move karne mein madad milti hai. Isliye recharge ek reversible process hai, magar perfect nahi: har reaction hamesha bilkul pehle jaisi condition restore nahi karti. Modern lithium-ion cell ko lithium metal ka dabba samajhna bhi theek nahi. Aam rechargeable designs lithium ko electrode materials ke structure mein store karte hain. Exact materials manufacturer aur battery chemistry ke hisaab se badal sakte hain.

Isko do mohallon ke darmiyan daily commute ki misaal se samjhein. Ions andar ke allowed raaste se jate hain, electrons external road lete hain aur is safar se kaam hota hai. Analogy ki had bhi yaad rakhein: ions chhote passengers nahi aur energy koi alag suitcase nahi. Chemical potential aur electrical forces asli mechanism hain. Model samajhne ke liye hai, molecule ki literal tasveer nahi.

2. Ek Nobel Prize tak pahunchne wali kahani

2019 ka Chemistry Nobel Prize John B. Goodenough, M. Stanley Whittingham aur Akira Yoshino ko lithium-ion batteries ki development ke liye mila. Nobel Prize ki official explanation ke mutabiq 1970s mein Whittingham ne titanium disulfide cathode aur lithium metal anode wala rechargeable design develop kiya. Isne useful voltage diya, magar reactive lithium metal safety challenge tha. Ek working idea aur everyday pocket mein rakhne layak product ke darmiyan kaafi engineering baqi thi.

1980 mein Goodenough ke cobalt oxide cathode ne qareeban chaar volt tak ki performance ka raasta khola. Yoshino ne 1985 mein carbon-based anode ke saath commercially viable lithium-ion battery ki bunyaad rakhi, jisme reactive metallic lithium ki zaroorat nahi thi. Official Nobel account commercial market mein aane ka saal 1991 batata hai. Yaani smartphone ki aadat ke peeche kai decades ki research hai, kisi ek company ki ek launch event wali invention nahi.

3. Battery buddhi kyun hoti hai?

Purane phone ki 100 percent charge par bhi runtime kam ho sakti hai. Percentage us waqt ki usable capacity ka andaaza hai, nayi battery ki guaranteed original energy nahi. Time aur use ke saath side reactions, electrode changes aur active lithium ki availability mein kami battery ko age kar sakti hai. Department of Energy ki battery research isi degradation ko samajhne aur kam karne par kaam karti hai. Har charging session turant battery ko kharab nahi karta, lekin chemistry hamesha jawan bhi nahi rehti.

Cycle ko sirf plug lagane ki ginti na samjhein. Partial discharges aur recharges mil kar cumulative use banate hain. Heat bhi aham factor hai. Garmi mein dashboard par phone chhorna, charging ke dauran blanket mein daba dena ya cooling ko block karna achhi aadat nahi. Apple ki battery guidance khas taur par high ambient temperature se bachne ko kehti hai. Har phone ke liye exact same lifespan ya cycle count quote karna sahi nahi, kyunki cell chemistry, software aur usage sab alag ho sakte hain.

4. Care mein common sense, na ke jadoo

Apne manufacturer ki charging guidance follow karein aur compatible, reliable charger use karein. Har roz battery ko zero tak gira kar phir full charge karna lithium-ion ke liye koi lazmi ritual nahi. Optimized charging features available hon to unhe samajh kar use kiya ja sakta hai. Kisi universal “80 percent ke upar ek baar gaye to battery barbaad” rule par yaqeen na karein. Charging control device aur battery management system ka bhi kaam hai.

Apple long-term storage ke liye qareeban half charge ki advice deta hai, na bilkul empty na indefinitely full; exact instructions device ke mutabiq dekhein. Battery swollen ho, unusual heat ya damage ho to use press, puncture ya ghar par repair na karein. Safe professional service lein. Battery care ka maqsad percentage se obsessive hona nahi: garmi se bachana, safe accessories aur abnormal signs ko serious lena zyada useful hai. Phone ki chhoti battery humein sikhati hai ke useful technology chemistry, materials aur careful design ka mil kar bana hua nateeja hoti hai.
