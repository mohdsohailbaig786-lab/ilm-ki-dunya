DART: kya hum asteroid ka raasta badal sakte hain?

25 November 2026 · 4 min padhai

Sochiye, space mein ek chattan apne raaste par ja rahi hai. Kya insaan uski chaal badal sakta hai? Film mein jawab aksar ek bada dhamaka hota hai. Asal science ka jawab zyada dilchasp hai: sahi jagah par spacecraft ki takkar. NASA ka DART, yani Double Asteroid Redirection Test, isi idea ka amli imtehan tha. Yeh kisi aane wali tabahi ki emergency nahi, balki planetary defence ki tayari ka controlled experiment tha.

Asteroid asal mein kya hai?

NASA ke mutabiq asteroids pathreele, baghair hawa ke ajraam hain jo taqreeban 4.6 arab saal pehle Solar System banne ke daur se bache hue hain. Zyada tar Mars aur Jupiter ke darmiyan main asteroid belt mein Suraj ke gird ghoomte hain. Har asteroid ek jaisa nahi hota. Unki banawat aur satah ko samajhna zaroori hai, kyunki takkar ka nateeja sirf spacecraft ki speed se tay nahi hota.

Ek chhota chaand, ek bada test

DART ne 26 September 2022 ko Dimorphos se jaan-boojh kar takkar maari. NASA ki mission overview mein is chhote asteroid moonlet ka diameter taqreeban 160 metre diya gaya hai. Yeh taqreeban 780 metre chaude Didymos ke gird ghoomta hai. Yani diye gaye diameters ke hisaab se bada saathi lagbhag paanch guna chauda tha; yeh mass ka comparison nahi. NASA ne saaf bataya ke is controlled test se pehle ya baad dono se Zameen ko khatra nahi tha.

32 minute ki kahani

Kamyabi naapne ka tareeqa bohot khoobsurat tha: Dimorphos apne saathi ka chakkar kitne waqt mein lagata hai? NASA ke 11 October 2022 ke ibtidai elan ke mutabiq, pehle yeh waqt 11 ghante 55 minute tha. Takkar ke baad measurement 11 ghante 23 minute nikli: orbit period 32 minute chhota hua. Is shuruati measurement ki uncertainty taqreeban plus ya minus 2 minute thi. Yeh us waqt ka reported nateeja hai, hamesha ke liye aakhri precise figure nahi.

Ghaur karein: yahan 32 minute ka matlab Zameen se takraane ka waqt taalna nahi. Yeh Dimorphos ke Didymos ke gird ek chakkar ki muddat ka farq hai. Scientists ne Zameen ke telescopes se observations lekar is tabdeeli ko naapa. Mission ka minimum successful period-change benchmark 73 second tha; NASA ke mutabiq ibtidai nateeja usse 25 guna se bhi zyada tha.

Dhakka sirf spacecraft ne nahi diya

NASA ne collision speed taqreeban 22,530 kilometre per hour batayi. Magar kahani mein ek aur madadgaar tha: ejecta, yani takkar se uchhal kar nikla pathreeela material. Is material ke bahar nikalne ka recoil asteroid ko milne wale dhakke ko aur mazboot karta hai. NASA isko gubbare se hawa nikalne ki misaal se samjhata hai. Yeh nuclear blast nahi tha; kinetic impact mein chalti hui cheez ki momentum transfer hoti hai.

DART se yeh sabit nahi hota ke har size aur har banawat ke asteroid ke liye ek hi recipe kaafi hogi. Yeh ek method ka full-scale demonstration tha. Asal seekh hai: pehle asteroid ko dhoondo, uski orbit samjho, phir evidence se faisla karo. Space ki hifazat sirf taqat nahi, sahi measurement ka kaam bhi hai.

Aaj ka sawal: Agar uchhalta malba bhi dhakka badhata hai, to sakht chattan aur dheele pathron ke dher par takkar ka asar ek jaisa kyun hoga?
