Ilm Ki Dunya ke baare mein

Ilm kisi ek field, zubaan ya degree ka mohtaaj nahi. Ilm Ki Dunya India ke Hindi/Urdu samajhne wale readers ke liye Roman Urdu aur Hinglish mein ek dostana knowledge website hai. Yahan science, technology, history, jism aur sehat, hairat-angez facts aur Islam aur adab ke topics milte hain.

Aasaan zubaan, mazboot bunyaad

Har article research-based hai. Hum space ke liye NASA, sehat ke liye NIH, samandar ke liye NOAA aur tareekh ke liye university aur heritage sources jaise bharosemand idaaron ko dekhte hain. Sources article ke aakhir mein linked hain. Number ko uske context ke saath pesh karna, estimate ko estimate kehna aur mashhoor ghalat fehmi ko durust karna hamare liye aham hai.

Har roz naya article

Yeh ek growing collection hai aur daily updates hamara editorial maqsad hain. Articles insaani editing aur research ke saath add kiye jayenge; website koi automatic news feed nahi. Starter collection ke sab articles ki tareekh 24 November 2026 hai.

Kaise padhein?

Apni pasand ki category kholein, estimated reading time dekhein, phir aakhir ka “Aaj ka sawal” apne dost ya ghar walon se discuss karein. Reading time 150 alfaaz per minute ka andaaza hai, test ya deadline nahi. Sehat ke articles general education hain, personal medical advice nahi. Deeni articles aam mainstream Sunni taleem aur adab ke daire mein hain; personal masail ke liye qualified alim se poochhein.

Yahan koi analytics, external font ya framework nahi. Sirf halki HTML, CSS aur chhota shared navigation script hai. Khush aamdeed — mil kar seekhte hain.
