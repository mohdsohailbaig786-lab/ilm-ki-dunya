Amazon rainforest: duniya saans kis se leti hai

24 November 2026

Amazon ko aksar “duniya ke phephre” keh diya jata hai. Tasveer khoobsurat hai, lekin phir ek ghalat number saath aa jata hai: duniya ki 20 percent oxygen sirf isi forest se milti hai. National Geographic ki scientific explanation is popular claim ko misleading batati hai. Iska matlab Amazon ki ahmiyat kam nahi. Balki uski asli kahani aur rich hai: carbon storage, paani ka cycle, biodiversity aur lakhon kilometre mein phaili interconnected zindagi. Chaliye slogan ko side par rakh kar rainforest ki asal science samajhte hain.

1. Forest, basin aur river ek hi map nahi

WWF Amazon ke dense forests ke liye qareeban 2.2 million square miles ka scale deta hai, jo rounded conversion mein qareeban 57 lakh square kilometre banta hai. Amazon basin ka area isse bada, qareeban 2.7 million square miles ya 70 lakh square kilometre ke order ka hai. Dono numbers ko ek doosre ki jagah use karna theek nahi. Basin woh drainage region hai jiska paani river system ki taraf jata hai; uska har square kilometre ek jaisa dense forest nahi hota.

School map mein green patch bana dena aasaan hai, magar ground par rivers, floodplains, upland forest aur human settlements ka complex mosaic hota hai. Amazon sirf Brazil ka ek isolated jungle bhi nahi; region South America ke kai countries tak phaila hai. Isliye protection ki discussion mein borders, local communities aur connected river systems sab aate hain. Ek jagah ka land-use change doosri jagah ke paani aur habitat ko affect kar sakta hai.

Area figures source ki definition ke saath badal sakte hain: koi rainforest boundary count karta hai, koi biome aur koi whole basin. Science mein number ke saath noun bhi padhna padta hai. “57 lakh” aur “70 lakh” mein se ek ko viral quiz ka absolute correct answer bana dene se pehle poochhein ke sawal kis region ki baat kar raha hai.

2. Oxygen banti hai, magar poori bachi nahi rehti

Green plants photosynthesis mein carbon dioxide aur paani se sugars banate hain aur oxygen release karte hain. Is sense mein Amazon ke trees oxygen produce karte hain. Magar trees khud respiration bhi karte hain, aur dead leaves aur wood ko break down karne wale organisms oxygen consume karte hain. Gross production, ya total bani oxygen, aur net contribution, ya consumption ke baad bachi oxygen, ek cheez nahi.

National Geographic ke explainer mein mature forest ke oxygen balance ko samjhaya gaya hai: plant respiration aur decomposition gross production ka bahut bada hissa use kar lete hain. Long-term net contribution ko atmospheric oxygen ka seedha 20 percent source kehna sahi nahi. Isi liye Amazon gayab ho to “kal duniya ki oxygen ka paanchwan hissa band ho jayega” wali image scientific account nahi. Lekin is correction se destruction harmless nahi ho jati.

Ek dukaan ka example lein: din bhar ek lakh rupaye ki sales aur expenses ke baad actual profit alag amounts hain. Photosynthesis aur respiration bilkul accounting business nahi, lekin gross aur net ka farq samajhne mein analogy kaam deti hai. Amazon ki value ko defend karne ke liye oxygen ka inflated claim zaroori nahi. Climate aur biodiversity ke real stakes khud bahut bade hain.

3. Paani aur carbon ki chalti hui machine

Roots se liya hua paani leaves ke zariye atmosphere mein wapas ja sakta hai; is process ko transpiration kehte hain. Evaporation ke saath mil kar yeh moisture transport aur rainfall patterns mein role ada karta hai. Forest ko sirf baarish se paani lene wala passive sponge samajhna adhoora hai: woh regional water cycle ko influence bhi karta hai. Zyada forest loss se drying aur fire vulnerability ka risk badh sakta hai, jo phir forest ko aur stress de sakta hai.

WWF Amazon forests mein stored carbon ka estimate 150–200 billion tonnes ke order mein deta hai. Estimate ka lafz yaad rakhein: har tree ka laboratory weighing nahi hua. Forest carbon stocks ko biomass aur doosri measurements se assess kiya jata hai. Jab trees burn ya decompose hote hain, stored carbon ka hissa atmosphere mein ja sakta hai. Isliye forest protection ka climate connection sirf naye trees ki annual growth nahi, pehle se stored carbon ko bachana bhi hai.

4. Species ke numbers ke peeche living systems

WWF ke mutabiq duniya ki known species mein se qareeban ek-daswan hissa Amazon region se associated hai. “Known” zaroori qualifier hai: biodiversity ke kai parts abhi document nahi hue. Britannica bhi rainforest ki extraordinary variety samjhata hai. Jaguars, monkeys, tapirs aur capybaras jaise familiar animals ke saath insects, fungi, plants aur microscopic organisms bhi ecosystem ka hissa hain. Sirf bade photogenic animals ka census poore jungle ki health nahi batata.

Indigenous peoples aur doosri local communities ko landscape se alag museum label ki tarah nahi dekhna chahiye. Conservation mein livelihoods, knowledge aur rights ka context aham hai. Deforestation aur fires ki discussion ko sirf ek dramatic satellite photo se complete nahi kiya ja sakta. Land use, governance aur ecological evidence ko saath samajhna padta hai. Ek reader ke liye pehla useful step reliable information aur misleading shortcuts ke darmiyan farq karna hai.

Amazon humein sikhata hai ke ecosystem ki value ek number mein band nahi hoti. Agar 20-percent-oxygen claim durust nahi, tab bhi carbon, water, habitats aur living diversity ki poori duniya baqi hai. Achhi environmental education ka kaam bas darana nahi, system ko samajhne layak banana hai.
