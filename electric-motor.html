Electric motor: bijli se ghoomti hui taqat

27 November 2026 · 2 min padhai

Pankhe ki hawa ke peeche motor hai. Motor electrical energy ko mechanical rotation mein badalti hai; hawa blades ke ghoomne se chalti hai.

Magnetic field ka dhakka

Current le jane wale conductor par magnetic field mein force lag sakti hai. Coil ke mukhtalif hisson par forces mil kar torque, yani ghumane ka asar, paida karti hain. Rotor ghoomne wala hissa hai aur stator apni jagah rehta hai. Motor ko current aur munasib magnetic arrangement dono chahiye.

Ghoomna jari kaise rehta hai?

Simple brushed DC motor mein commutator coil ke current ki direction munasib waqt par badalta hai, taake torque ek hi taraf rahe. Har motor mein brushes nahi hote. Aam AC induction motor mein stator ka changing field rotor mein current induce karta hai. BLDC fan mein electronic controller switching karta hai. Is liye ek simple model sab motors ka poora naqsha nahi.

Fan aur energy ka hisaab

Motor fan blades ko ghumati hai, blades hawa ko movement deti hain. Kuch energy bearings ke friction, winding ke resistance aur doosre losses se heat ban jati hai. Agar fan jam ho to switch band karein; motor ko haath se chhed kar chalane ki koshish na karein. Mains electricity jaanleva ho sakti hai. Yeh samajhne ka mazmoon hai, wiring ya repair ki hidayat nahi; trained electrician se kaam karwayein.

Aaj ka sawal: Agar bearing mein friction badh jaye to heating aur energy use par kya asar padega?

Apna ilm test karo

Pehle khud sochein, phir jawab khol kar jaanchein.

1. Motor kis energy ko rotation mein badalti hai?
Jawab dekhein

Electrical energy.

2. Rotor kya hai?
Jawab dekhein

Ghoomne wala hissa.

3. Kya har motor mein brushes hote hain?
Jawab dekhein

Nahin; induction aur brushless designs bhi hote hain.
