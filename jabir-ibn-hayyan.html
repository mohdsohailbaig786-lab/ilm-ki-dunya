Jabir ibn Hayyan: chemistry ki tareekh ko samjhein

27 November 2026 · 2 min padhai

Laboratory mein alag karna, garam karna aur natije note karna aham hai. Jabir se mansoob kitaabein hamein early Arabic alchemy ki isi duniya se milwati hain.

Tareekh mein ehtiyat

Jabir ibn Hayyan ko riwayatan aathwin aur nauwin sadi CE ke Kufa, Iraq se jora jata hai; aam tareekhein taqreeban 721 se 815 CE hain. Magar unki zindagi ki tafseel aur unse mansoob bohot si kitabon ki authorship par historians mein bahas hai. Mumkin hai corpus ka khaasa hissa baad ke musannifeen ka ho. Unhein Muslim ilmi tareekh mein padhein, magar har qissa yaqini waqia na samjhein.

Alchemy aur chemistry ka farq

Alchemy mein madon ki tabdeeli ke saath aise nazariyat bhi the jo aaj ki science qabool nahi karti. Jabirian riwayat mein distillation, crystallisation aur doosre laboratory processes ki bahas milti hai. Distillation mein liquid ko bhaap bana kar phir thanda karke jama kiya jata hai; har mixture ke liye yeh tareeqa ek jaisa kaam nahi karta.

Baad ke ilm par asar

Arabic aur Latin alchemical literature ne laboratory zubaan aur techniques ki riwayat aage pahunchai. Latin naam Geber se chalne wali har kitaab ko seedha Jabir ki tasneef kehna durust nahi. Unki kahani ki achhi seekh hai: source, tareekh aur saboot ko saath dekhein. Yeh ghar par acid banane ya chemical experiment karne ki hidayat nahi; chemicals aur heat ke tajurbe trained supervision maangte hain.

Aaj ka sawal: Kisi purani kitaab par mashhoor naam likha ho to authorship ka saboot aur kyun chahiye?
